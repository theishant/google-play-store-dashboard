📊 Google Play Store Analytics Dashboard

Task 1: Sentiment Distribution

Visualizes user sentiment (positive, neutral, negative) by rating groups for apps with over 1,000 reviews in the top 5 categories.

Task 2: Average Installs vs Revenue

Dual-axis chart comparing installs and revenue for Free vs Paid apps within top 3 categories. Applies strict filters.

Task 3: Ratings & Reviews Comparison

Grouped bar chart comparing average rating and total reviews for top 10 categories by installs. Applies strict filters.

Task 4: Global Installs Choropleth

Interactive world map showing installs by category for top 5 categories only, highlighting installs above 1 million.

Task 5: Size vs Rating Bubble Chart

Bubble chart showing app size vs average rating, bubble size = installs, category translations included.

Task 6: Time Series of Installs

Time series chart for installs trend over time, highlighting growth spikes and category translations.
